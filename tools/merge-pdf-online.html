Time calculator — add hours and minutes, or subtract them, with proper base-60 carrying, plus the decimal-hour total for payroll.

Common questions about the Time Calculator

What is 2 hours 45 minutes plus 1 hour 30 minutes?

4 hours 15 minutes — 45 + 30 minutes is 75 minutes, which carries as 1 hour 15 minutes. As decimal hours that is 4.25 hours, which the Time Calculator shows alongside the hours-and-minutes total.

Why does a normal calculator get hours and minutes wrong?

Because minutes carry at 60, not 100: 2.30 + 1.45 on a desk calculator gives 3.75, but 2 hours 30 minutes plus 1 hour 45 minutes is really 4 hours 15 minutes. This calculator carries minutes at 60.

How do I convert hours and minutes to decimal hours?

Divide the minutes by 60 and add the hours: 4 hours 15 minutes is 4 + 15/60 = 4.25 hours. The Time Calculator shows that decimal total with every result.

The decimal-hour total is the payroll number: 4 hours 15 minutes is 4.25 hours, not 4.15. Subtracting a longer duration from a shorter one gives a negative total, shown with a minus sign.
